V7 icon assets

WhatsApp: WhatsApp icon asset sourced from the WhatsApp/Meta brand asset lineage via Wikimedia Commons (File: WhatsApp Logo green.svg).
Official guidance: https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/whatsapp/whatsapp-brand/

Phone, Email, Location: Google Material Icons style PNG assets are included locally so they render consistently without external font loading.
Official Google Material Icons documentation: https://developers.google.com/fonts/docs/material_icons

All icons are bundled locally in /icons so the website does not depend on Google, Meta, or third-party image loading at runtime.
